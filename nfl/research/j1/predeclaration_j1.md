# J1 pre-registration — the joint rushing-opportunity architecture

Written 2026-09-09, **before any candidate was fitted or scored**. Start HEAD
`1af5b18`. Departures are labelled where they occur.

Authorised by owner ruling 2026-09-09: *"You are authorized to design and
execute a new preregistered research experiment for the correct joint
rushing-opportunity architecture... Do not assume the answer is 'carve QB
first.'"*

---

## 0. A root defect found while scoping this, which changes the design

The owner's arrow was `team carries → QB rush + RB/FB + WR/TE residual`. Scoping
it surfaced something upstream that the arrow presupposes and that is not true
today.

**`team_volume_v1.forecast` draws its five team metrics independently.** The
code is `for metric in METRICS:` with
`rng = np.random.default_rng([seed, ordinal, hash(metric) % 9973])` — a separate
stream per metric, residuals resampled from separate pools.

Measured, 3,230 team-games:

| correlation | historical (within team) | D1 draws |
|---|---|---|
| team_carries vs team_dropbacks_part | **−0.393** | **−0.004** |
| team_carries vs team_targets | **−0.388** | **+0.007** |

The dominant joint structure in team football — a team that ends up passing runs
less — is **absent from the draws**. A single draw can put a team at the 90th
percentile of both dropbacks and carries, which is a snap count that does not
occur. Every downstream player quantity inherits it: a receiver's targets and a
back's carries are effectively independent across draws when in reality they
trade off.

This is the owner's own permanent constraint — *"make independent draws when the
football quantities must be coupled"* — and the QB-rush/OTHER incoherence this
packet was sent to fix is one symptom of it, one level down.

**Consequence for the design:** a rushing factorization cannot be evaluated
honestly while `team_carries` is itself drawn incoherently with
`team_dropbacks`, because **scrambles are generated by the dropback process and
are counted as rush attempts**. The experiment therefore covers the team split,
not only the within-carries allocation. This is a widening of the owner's arrow
and it is declared here rather than done quietly.

## 1. The measured structure the architecture must respect

All on 3,230 team-games, 2020–2025.

**QB rush decomposes exactly.** `scrambles + designed = QB carries` in
**3,230 of 3,230**. The two behave oppositely:

| | corr with team carries | corr with team dropbacks |
|---|---|---|
| scrambles | +0.180 | +0.183 |
| designed rushes | **+0.440** | **−0.218** |

Designed rushes are a run-game quantity. Scrambles are a **passing**-game
by-product that lands in the rushing column.

**The accounting spine.**

- `team_carries − scrambles = designed runs`, and designed runs
  `= RB/FB + WR/TE + QB designed` exactly in **3,148 of 3,230** (97.5%; the
  residue is penalty and lateral edge cases already named elsewhere in this
  repository).
- `team_off_snaps ≈ team_dropbacks + designed runs`, mean gap **+3.07** snaps
  (sd 1.95). Not an exact identity — kneels, spikes and no-plays sit in the gap
  — so it is used as a **soft** constraint and never enforced by clipping.
- Both components load positively on snaps (dropbacks +0.628, designed +0.383)
  while trading off against each other (−0.452). **Generating the split
  reproduces the −0.39 coupling; generating the two totals separately destroys
  it.**

**Decomposition of team carries:** RB/FB 0.8082, QB 0.1568 (scrambles 0.0672 +
designed 0.0896), WR/TE 0.0340, unattributed 0.0010.

## 2. Estimand

The **joint predictive distribution, on one draw index, of**

```
  (team_dropbacks, QB_scrambles, QB_designed, RB_FB_carries, WR_TE_carries)
```

with `team_carries` **derived** as `QB_scrambles + QB_designed + RB_FB + WR_TE`
rather than drawn.

Per-player allocation within RB/FB remains the frozen P4C simplex and is **not**
re-estimated here. This experiment is about the *group* factorization.

## 3. Architectures compared

**A0 — control, the status quo.** Team metrics drawn independently (D1 as it
stands); P4C carries simplex over the drawn `team_carries`; QB rush drawn
independently by the QB layer. This is what production does today and it is the
control precisely so the size of the defect is scored rather than described.

**A1 — carve QB first.** Draw QB rush, subtract from drawn `team_carries`,
allocate the RB simplex over the remainder. The obvious candidate; the owner
explicitly declined to assume it.

**A2 — causal split.** Draw `team_off_snaps`; split into dropbacks and designed
runs; draw `scrambles ~ Binomial(dropbacks, player scramble rate)`; allocate
designed runs across RB/FB, QB-designed and WR/TE as a simplex with explicit
named residual; derive `team_carries`.

**A3 — joint residual resampling.** Keep every D1 marginal fit exactly as frozen
and change **only** the draw: resample the five metric residuals as a **vector
indexed by the same historical team-game**, so the observed cross-metric
correlation is carried without altering any marginal. Minimal change, and it is
included because a minimal change that works should beat a redesign that also
works.

## 4. Chronology

Walk-forward. For evaluation season *Y*, every rate, pool and residual comes
from seasons `< Y`; within-season history uses a strictly-earlier ordinal prefix
cut (`bisect`, not append-as-we-go). Evaluation seasons **2022, 2023, 2024,
2025**. A fold whose inputs are unavailable is reported as skipped with its
cause and is **never averaged in as a `nan`** — that defect occurred in QB3 and
is not repeated.

**No 2026 outcome is consumed.** `PATH_C_STATE` is not modified.

## 5. Scoring, fixed now

**Marginal quality** — CRPS per team-game for each of `team_dropbacks`,
`team_carries`, `RB_FB_carries`, `QB_rush`; and MAE alongside.

**Joint quality, which is the point of the experiment** — the primary metric is
the **energy score**, a strictly proper multivariate score, on the vector
`(team_dropbacks, team_carries, QB_rush, RB_FB_carries)`. Reported alongside:
the reproduced within-team correlation matrix against the historical one, and
the absolute error in `corr(team_carries, team_dropbacks)`.

**Accounting invariants**, counted per draw and reported as rates:

1. `QB_scrambles + QB_designed + RB_FB + WR_TE == team_carries` (exact)
2. `QB_rush <= team_carries`
3. every component `>= 0`
4. `QB_scrambles <= team_dropbacks`
5. the named residual mass is explicit and non-negative

**No clipping is permitted as a substitute for modelling.** An architecture that
requires a clip to satisfy an invariant is recorded as failing that invariant,
and the count of clipped draws is reported for every arm — including A0, whose
`negative_draws_clipped_to_zero` already exists in its metadata.

**Uncertainty:** team-game clustered bootstrap. Naive intervals are not
reported.

## 6. Acceptance rule, fixed before any result

A candidate is preferred over A0 only if **all** hold:

1. the energy score improves;
2. the team-game clustered interval on the energy-score difference excludes
   zero;
3. the direction is consistent in at least 3 of the folds that run;
4. no marginal CRPS worsens by more than 2% relative;
5. every accounting invariant holds at a rate no worse than A0's.

Between candidates that clear the bar, **the simplest is preferred**: A3 over
A2 over A1, in that order, unless a later arm beats an earlier one on criterion
1 with an interval excluding zero.

**If no candidate clears the bar**, the return says the joint structure is not
recovered by any architecture tested and names what would be needed. A failure
to beat A0 on the energy score would be a surprising result and would be
reported as such rather than explained away.

## 7. What a result that replaces P4C would look like

The owner's ruling asks for this to be reported cleanly rather than forced into
backward compatibility. If A2 wins, the frozen P4C `carries` mass pool is fitted
against a denominator (`team_carries`) that A2 no longer draws, so **the
parameterization would have to be refitted against designed runs**. That is a
replacement of an accepted artifact and would be reported as requiring its own
owner ruling — this experiment would establish the case, not execute it.
