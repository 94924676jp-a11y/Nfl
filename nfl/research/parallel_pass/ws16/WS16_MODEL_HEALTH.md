## 0. The three rules this matrix obeys

1. **Tests passing is not calibration evidence.** No layer is marked healthy
   because its suite is green. `nfl/production/authorization.py` already says
   the same thing about publication — "there is no path from a green test
   suite to authorization" — and the same applies to health.
2. **A status is a statement about measured evidence.** Where no outcome
   measurement exists for a layer, the status is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and
   the experiment that would produce some is named. It is never inferred from
   the layer's design, its docstring, or its governance token.
3. **Governance tokens are not evidence.** `layers.SPEC` carries strings like
   `SIGNAL_WEAK`, `CALIBRATION_DEFECT`, `HOLD_TENTATIVE`. The DAL/NYG audit
   finding 9 established that these gate nothing — `state = PASS` means the
   stage ran. They are quoted here as declarations, never as measurements.

### Status vocabulary as used here

| STATUS | Means |
|---|---|
| `STRONG_PRESERVE` | an outcome measurement exists, it is good on its own estimand, and the risk is degrading it by touching it |
| `ADEQUATE_MONITOR` | an outcome measurement exists and is acceptable, with a named caveat that could turn it |
| `WEAK_RESEARCH` | measured, and measured poorly; the mechanism is not obviously broken, the signal is small |
| `SPECIFICATION_DEFECT` | an accepted audit names a mechanism that is wrong in kind, not merely imprecise |
| `INPUT_DEFECT` | the layer is specified correctly and is fed the wrong quantity |
| `DATA_BLOCKED` | the layer cannot run or cannot be evaluated because bytes that exist in the world are not in this checkout |
| `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` | no calibration evidence exists for this layer at all |

## 10. What we are best at, and worst at — grounded in numbers

### Best

1. **Participation share.** `ewma_hl2`, pooled **r = 0.8198**, MAE 0.1294, SD ratio 0.887 over **22,500** player-games with ≥99.8% coverage in every season, beating `last_observation` (0.797) and `position_mean` (0.285). The caveat is load-bearing and is not a hedge: the estimand is an **upper bound** on route participation, and the gap to routes run is directional with **unbounded** magnitude.
2. **Team volume point calibration.** Against authoritative PBP on 18 team-games: carries bias **+0.037 (z = +0.02)**, targets **+0.128 (z = +0.07)**. A defect hypothesis was raised against this layer and **falsified**. Its own research says the quantity is close to unforecastable (P4 correlation peaked at 0.167) and the layer's job is to represent that honestly rather than to beat it — which it does.
3. **Accounting and integrity.** Seven HARD accounting invariants plus five HARD draw invariants; closure exact to **2.4e-07** in the C1 evaluation and exact in **2,174 of 2,174** team-games in `own5`. This layer caught D04 — an inactive QB holding 0.90 dropbacks in the mean and **22.6%** in the tail — because it checks the max over draws rather than the mean.
4. **Refusal discipline.** 115 sealed forecasts, 0 scored, every refusal named. `rushing_conversion` has refused to invent `carries × ypc` for the entire life of the project. Three of five team quantities are refused **by name** rather than scored on an estimand that does not match.

### Worst

1. **QB participation, and it is a specification defect, not noise.** Conditional on an established incumbent with no planned rotation, the starter took **zero dropbacks in 0 of 1,700 team-games** (2021–2024). The production allocator assigns that event **7.36%** of the mass in its cleanest cell. One bit — who started the club's week-18 game *last season*, resolved by an ordinal that crosses the season boundary — is worth **35 points** of Dak Prescott's dropbacks. The consequence downstream: corr(QB1, QB2) = **−0.9240**, P(exactly one QB exceeds 10 dropbacks) = **0.9795**.
2. **Appearance is sign-inverted in live use.** The two players on the official inactive list carried the roster's two **highest** appearance probabilities (0.9895 and 0.9465) while the RB1 sat at 0.6580. Development AUC of 0.859–0.869 did not survive contact with a cold-start player and a carried-absence field that is unused or wrong-signed.
3. **The carry allocator subtracts the non-RB mass twice.** Fitted `other` = **0.198875** on the team denominator; correct residual on the RB denominator = **0.008754**; overstated **22.7×**, removing **0.1901** of the RB budget a second time. Graded live: carries bias **−2.909**, cov50 **0.417**, cov80 **0.750**, both below nominal. The repair is measured (pooled CRPS 2.171 → 1.849, clustered CI [−0.336, −0.309]) and is **development-only, oracled, and not promoted**.
4. **Model-versus-market disagreement is anticalibrated.** Of 11 health metrics, **10 are ranking-ineligible** and the only survivor has no market rows. The largest gaps were the worst rows: rushing attempts at a **26.04 pp** mean gap went **3–8**. The model picks the under on **80.6%–100%** of comparisons depending on the family — a property of the model before it is a property of the market.
5. **Efficiency is nearly unforecastable from held inputs, and this is the structural finding.** Conversion prior-to-next-game r = **0.083** against opportunity r = **0.591**. The whole RC1 ladder recovered **4.89%** of the conversion oracle, worth **0.96%** of baseline CRPS. TD composition improvement CI **spans zero**. This matches W2's headline for the QB layer: prior-form r = 0.136, the same model given realised dropbacks r = 0.636.
6. **Discrimination is compressed everywhere it is measured.** SD ratios of model to actual: targets 0.724, receptions 0.665, receiving yards 0.635, carries 0.648. Report those with r (0.689, 0.625, 0.612, 0.786) and the OLS slopes (0.952, 0.941, 0.964, 1.213) together — as the repo's own CLAUDE.md insists, they are one fact in three forms, and the SD ratio is not a dial.

## 12. Evidence ceiling

**No admissible prospective evidence unit exists in this repository today.**
That is the ceiling, and every status above sits under it.

1. **The prospective ledger is not evidence.** `PROSPECTIVE_LEDGER.jsonl` holds
   **3,230 rows** spanning exactly **two games** (`2026_01_SF_LA` 3,130 rows,
   `2026_01_NE_SEA` 100) across **five** candidate configurations
   (`V1_CANDIDATE`, `_R5`, `_R6`, `_R7`, `_R8`) of the same games. 1,570 rows
   carry `cutoff_regime = UNLABELLED`; every row has `promoted = false`; no row
   carries a layer, season, admissibility or evidence-unit field. These rows
   **predate the admissibility stamp and count toward no evidence unit.**
   Candidate variants of one game are paired comparisons on that game, not
   independent games — `postgame.py` says so itself.
2. **The 2026-09-13 slate audit is diagnostic, by its own declaration.** 415
   rows, 9 sealed games (a tenth, NE@SEA, skipped `NO_PREGAME_SEAL`), split
   268 non-contaminated / 147 QB3-contaminated. Its governance line: "DIAGNOSTIC
   ONLY. Not prospective evidence, not a ledger row, does not count toward any
   sample floor, and clears no blocker." Its limits: "one slate is not a sample…
   nothing here is clustered and no interval is quoted… a forecast that was
   wrong today is not thereby a defect."
3. **115 sealed forecasts, 0 scored.** Every 2026 seal is refused
   `POSTGAME_ARTIFACT_NOT_CURRENTLY_ADMISSIBLE` on 3 mandatory controls. The
   gate is correct and it is the reason the denominator is zero.
4. **All historical evidence is development data and is labelled so.** QB3
   (2,685 team-games), P4C, RC1 (22,521 rows / 1,087 games), TD2 (16,976 rows),
   C1 (1,630 clustered team-games) each carry `EXPLORATORY` or
   "heavily mined development data". None of it can be the left-hand side of a
   confirmatory comparison. C1 additionally **oracles team volume**, declared.
5. **The nominal n is inflated by 3–10× wherever more than one line per
   player-game is graded.** C2 measured a ladder-row design effect of **6.76**;
   the clustered-to-naive SE ratio runs **3 to 10**. Every hit rate and every
   coverage figure in section 9 is quoted without clustering, because the
   artifacts quote them without clustering.
6. **Two named data ceilings bound layers 5, 11 and 12.** The injury feed
   carries **no publication clock** — `both blank` is 0 across all seven
   captures, so the proposed weaker guard would never fire and was withdrawn.
   And `PREDICTION_TIME_ELIGIBILITY_UNAVAILABLE` is open: the roster vintage has
   five columns and no status field, and the vendor's `status` is post-hoc
   (retrospective ACT predicts snaps at 0.9715, tautologically). Both are in
   `docs/AGENT_OUTBOX.md` because they need bytes from outside this checkout.
7. **Determinism is not currently proven.** Re-running the committed Q9 dry-run
   gives 9/10 with two seals differing on `artifact_id`, `forecast_id`,
   `seal_payload_sha256` and `identity_fingerprint`. Until that is resolved or
   withdrawn, "reproducible" is a claim this repository cannot fully support.

**What this means for reading the matrix.** Six layers are marked
`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and three of those are layers that do not exist at all
(defence, weather, game state). Two are marked `STRONG_PRESERVE` on the strength
of *structural* enforcement rather than outcome calibration — that is stated in
their rows and is not the same claim. Exactly **one** layer in the entire
architecture carries an outcome-graded result that is both good and on its own
declared estimand: participation. Everything else is either measured and weak,
measured and defective, or unmeasured.
